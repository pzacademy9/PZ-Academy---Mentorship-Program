# Contacts page, guided: what you need to do

## Database
Nothing to paste. There is no database update for this change.

## How to look at it
1. Open the Sales Hub, then Contacts (`/dashboard/admin/sales-hub/contacts`).
2. The page now has three steps: 1. Find people, 2. Pick people, 3. What do you want to do?
3. Type a name, email or phone, press Search, tick a few people, and see "N people selected" with the actions.

## What to check on a phone
- The page does not scroll sideways.
- The bottom bar does not cover the menu.
- The More menu opens fully and you can reach every item.
- The labels show no wrong characters (no strange symbols where a dash or "..." should be).

## What stays the same
- Every action does what it did before.
- Nobody is emailed by looking at the page. Emails only go out when you start a send yourself.
