# Guided Cohorts page: your steps

## Nothing to paste

There is no database change in this work. You do not need to paste any SQL.

## How to look at it

1. Sign in as admin and open Sales Hub, then the Cohorts tab.
2. You should see three numbered steps: "1. Find a cohort", "2. Pick cohorts" and "3. What do you want to do?".
3. Tick two cohorts. The third step should say "2 cohorts selected", show "Up to N people", and offer "Download for Meta" and "Download plain version".
4. On a big screen, the third step is a card beside the list that stays in view as you scroll. On a phone or tablet it is a bar above the bottom menu.

## Check on a phone

- The page does not slide sideways.
- The bottom bar does not cover the More menu of a cohort. Scroll if you need to; the bar never takes more than half the screen.
- Tap More on a cohort. The menu opens fully and you can reach Download for Meta, Download plain version and Delete.
- Dates and words show normally, with no wrong characters (no strange symbols in place of letters).

## Check the browser console

Open the Cohorts page, then open the browser's developer tools (F12) and look at the Console. There should be no red error on load. The red error you saw before was the date problem, which is fixed: dates now always print in Pakistan time.

## What has not changed

- Downloading for Meta and the plain version works as before, and the files are the same.
- Deleting a cohort works as before and still asks you first.
- The limit of 20 cohorts at a time is the same.
