# Guided Audiences page: what you do

## Nothing to paste

This change has no database update. There is nothing to run in Supabase.

## How to look at it (after it is live)

1. Open Audiences in the admin area.
2. Step 1, Who: choose a course, Recorded or Live, country and payment. A sentence always says what you chose, with the number of people.
3. Press Next. Step 2, Check: you see a few people from the list. Their emails are partly hidden. It also says how many cannot go to Meta and why.
4. Press Next. Step 3, Download: you see the file name, how many people it holds, and which columns it has, before you download anything.
5. Press Back at any point. Your choices stay.

## What to check on your phone

- No sideways scrolling on any step.
- The number bar at the bottom does not cover the menu.
- No full email address shows on screen.

## Good to know

- No one is emailed. Nothing is written. The preview only reads.
- The saved lists, Bring in and the downloaded files work as before.
