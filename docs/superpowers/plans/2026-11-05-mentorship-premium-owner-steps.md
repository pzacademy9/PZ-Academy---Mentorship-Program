# Premium Mentorship page + mentor application: owner steps

Branch `mentorship-page`. No database step: nothing to paste.
Built from the Stitch "1-on-1 Mentorship" and "Mentorship" designs (Platform 2).

## Try it on the Vercel preview

1. Open /mentorship on your phone and on a computer.
   - Top: "Find your mentor", search box, and (on a computer) a ring of your real mentor photos.
   - Three cards: number of verified mentors, number of fields, payment checked within 24 hours.
     These are real counts from your mentors. No made-up numbers.
   - Field buttons and "N mentors available". Tap a field: only those mentors show. Search a name.
   - Top mentors (only when some mentors have a top badge), then All mentors.
   - Each card: photo with a verified tick, name, title, field and skills, star rating only when the
     mentor has real reviews, "From PKR … / session", when they are available, View profile, Book session.
   - How it works (4 steps), Share your clinical expertise, Looking for group learning instead?
2. Check every mentor card shows the right price and photo. If a card has no "available" line,
   add "Availability" text to that mentor in the admin.
3. Press **Start my application**. On /mentorship/apply fill the form with a test name and send it.
   - CV: PDF or Word under 2 MB. Photo: it is made smaller by itself; it must end under 1 MB.
   - Check it appears in Admin, Mentorship, Applications, and that the email arrives.
   - Reject the test application afterwards.
4. The mentor profile and booking pages still have the old look. They are the next branch.
