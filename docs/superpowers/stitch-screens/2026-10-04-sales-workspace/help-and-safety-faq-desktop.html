Honest Note on Safety Limits #ProtectsYourSIM

WhatsApp does not publish its limits, so we stay well under what we think is safe. Pacing protects your personal phone line so you never lose contact with your prospective students.

contact_support Frequent Safety Inquiries

5 Clear Answers

If you send 20 messages in one minute, WhatsApp thinks you are a computer spamming people and might restrict your SIM.

The 90 to 180 second pause between sends makes your activity look like a real, thoughtful human typing to a colleague. Take a sip of water — your line stays protected!

local_cafe Pacing suggestion: Review candidate transcripts during cooldowns.

Standard active numbers can send up to 60 new prospect chats per day and 20 per hour.

If your number is brand new (warming up), your daily allowance starts at 10 and increases gently every day.

check_circle Existing ongoing conversations with students you already talk to do not count against this limit.

After every 10 WhatsApp messages you send, the CRM gently pauses your queue for 10 minutes.

This mimics natural human work habits, lets prospective candidates read your notes, and keeps WhatsApp's automated anti-spam algorithms calm.

warning Immediate Safety Procedure

If WhatsApp shows you a security prompt or warning, stop messaging immediately! Hit the red "Something is wrong, stop" panic button on your screen.

This freezes the number for 48 hours to cool down and alerts your IT administrator right away without penalizing your sales quota.

For lead reassignment, cohort timetable inquiries, or SIM swap issues, contact your Senior Admissions Coordinator (Elena Vance) or use the #admissions-help Slack channel.

Technical CRM issues are handled directly by IT Support via ticket or rapid Slack ping.
