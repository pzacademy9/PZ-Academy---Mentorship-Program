## DOCUMENT 1: Affiliate Terms and Conditions

**Last updated:** [date of publishing]

These terms are an agreement between you and Pharmacozyme, which runs PZ Academy ("we", "us"). By ticking the box on
the application form, or by using your affiliate code or link, you agree to them.

### 1. Who can join
You must be at least [18] years old and able to make a legal agreement. We choose who joins. We may accept or decline
any application without giving a reason.

### 2. Your role
You are an independent affiliate. You are not our employee, agent, partner or representative. You cannot sign
anything for us, make promises for us, or speak as PZ Academy. You choose how and when you promote, within these terms
and the Affiliate Rules.

### 3. Your code and your private link
- If we accept you, we give you a personal affiliate code and a private link. Keep your private link to yourself.
  Anyone who has it can see your numbers and request a payout.
- Your code is yours to share with students. It gives students [10%] off. A student may also type your code with a
  percent after it, such as `CODE-15%`, up to the highest percent we allow (currently 30%). A bigger discount lowers
  your commission, because commission is worked out on what the student actually pays.
- We may change your code if it is misused. Old codes we already issued keep working until we say otherwise.

### 4. Commission
- You earn commission of [8% to 10%] of the amount a student actually pays for a registration that uses your code or
  your link, after any discount. The percent is either set by us for you, or follows the automatic rule: 8%, or 10%
  for a whole month if you had 10 or more paid participants in the month before. Fewer than 10 in a month means 8%
  the next month. Months are counted in Pakistan time. We tell you which applies to you.
- A sale counts only when we have confirmed the payment as Paid. Registrations that are unpaid, free, cancelled,
  refunded or marked as a test earn nothing.
- If a payment is refunded or reversed after commission was counted, we take that commission back. If you have
  already been paid for it, we may deduct it from your next payout.
- A sale counts for you if the student uses your code, or registers through your private link within [30] days of
  opening it. If a student does both, the private link comes first.
- You may not earn commission on your own registration. Relatives and friends who register for themselves can earn
  you commission like anyone else, as long as they really are the ones registering and paying. Registering people
  under false details to earn commission is not allowed.
- We decide the final figures from our records. If you think a figure is wrong, write to us within [30] days and we
  will check it.
- We may change commission percents or rules for future sales. We will tell you before the change starts. Sales already
  counted are not changed.

### 5. Payouts
- You can ask for a payout when your balance is PKR [3,500] or more.
- When you ask, you tell us where to send the money (JazzCash, Easypaisa or a bank account). You must give accurate

- We aim to pay within [7] working days of a request. We pay by hand and mark the request as paid.
- You are responsible for any tax that applies to your earnings. We may ask for identity details before paying.
- If we cannot reach you or the payment details do not work, we may hold the payout until you fix it.

### 6. Rules you must follow
You must follow the Affiliate Rules (Document 2). Breaking them can lead to losing commission and being removed.

### 7. Promotion materials and our name
You may use our name and approved material to promote our courses honestly. You may not change our logo, copy our
website, or run paid advertising that uses our name or logo unless we agree in writing first.

### 8. Ending
- You can stop any time by telling us.
- We can drop you at any time, and without notice if you break the rules, cheat, or harm our name.
- When you stop or are dropped, you keep commission already earned and confirmed, except where it came from rule

### 9. No promises
We do not promise any amount of income, any number of students, or that the programme will continue. Courses, prices,

### 11. Changes to these terms

## DOCUMENT 2: Affiliate Rules (conduct and anti-spam)

These are short, plain rules. They are part of the Affiliate Terms.

1. **Be honest.** Say only true things about our courses, prices, certificates and discounts. Never promise jobs,
   exam results or income.
2. **No spam.** Do not send bulk or unwanted messages. Do not add people to groups without asking. Do not post your
   code over and over in groups where promotion is not allowed. Follow the rules of each platform and group.
3. **Respect privacy.** Never share another person's phone number or email. Never collect students' details for
   yourself. Send students to our registration page.
4. **Discounts only through your code.** Do not offer a bigger discount than the code gives, and do not promise
   discounts we have not approved. Do not share or sell your code or private link to others.
5. **Do not pretend to be us.** Do not say you work for PZ Academy, do not use our logo as if you were us, and do
   not make accounts or pages that look like ours.
6. **No cheating.** No fake registrations, no registering yourself to earn commission, no registering people under
   false details, no tricks to make a sale look like yours.
7. **Pay attention to students.** Answer their questions politely. Send payment or course problems to us, do not
   handle money yourself. Never collect payments for us.
8. **If you break the rules,** we may remove commission from the sales involved, pause or end your account, and
   tell the people affected.

## DOCUMENT 3: Affiliate Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [date of publishing]

### What we collect
- **From your application:** your name, email, WhatsApp number, city and country, whether you are a student or
  professional, your university or organization, your field, your answers about experience and skills, the hours you
  can give, an optional LinkedIn link, and why you want to join. We also record when you accepted the terms.
- **When you are an affiliate:** your code, your team colour (if any), the sales made with your code or link, your
  commission and payouts, and the payment details you give when you ask for a payout.
- **From your link:** we count visits to pages opened through your private link (without storing the visitor's name).

### Why we use it
To review your application, run the programme, pay you, keep a fair leaderboard, send you emails about your
account and results, and protect our programme from fraud and misuse.

### Who sees it
- Our team members who manage affiliates.
- Our email provider (Brevo), only to send you the emails about your account.
- Other affiliates and the public see only your first name, last initial, team colour and paid count on the
  leaderboard. They do not see your email, phone, city or money.
- We do not sell your details.

### How long we keep it
We keep your details while you are an affiliate and for [2] years after, for records and disputes, unless the law
asks us to keep them longer. You can ask us to delete your details sooner, except where we must keep them (for
example, payout records).

### Your choices
You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or leave the leaderboard, by writing to
affiliates@pharmacozyme.com. We will answer within [30] days.

### Security
We keep your details in protected systems and limit who can open them. No system is perfectly safe, so keep your
private link private.

### Changes and contact
We may update this policy and will show the new version on the affiliate pages. Contact: affiliates@pharmacozyme.com.
